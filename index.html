Scenario: Medical Disease Screening

We are solving for the probability that a patient actually has the disease given that they tested positive. This illustrates the "Base Rate Fallacy"—why a positive result for a rare disease might still mean you are likely healthy.

  • Hypothesis (\(H\)): The patient has the disease (vs. Healthy).
  • Evidence (\(E\)): The test result is Positive (vs. Negative).
  • Goal: Calculate \( P(Disease | Positive) \).
